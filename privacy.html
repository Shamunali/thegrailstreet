Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how The Grail Street Poster ("the app"), the internal publishing tool of The Grail Street, handles information. The app is used only by The Grail Street team with accounts The Grail Street owns.

Information we access

  • Account connection data: when we connect a TikTok, YouTube (Google) or Instagram account, the platform gives the app an access token and basic profile information (account name, ID and profile picture).
  • Content we publish: the videos, captions, titles and settings our team uploads for publishing.
  • Publishing status: post IDs and whether a post succeeded, returned by the platform.

We do not access followers' personal data, private messages or contacts, and we do not use any data for advertising.

How we use it

Only to publish and schedule The Grail Street's own content to The Grail Street's own accounts, and to show the status of those posts to our team.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored on a server controlled by The Grail Street. We do not sell, rent or share it with third parties. Data is sent only to the platform it belongs to (TikTok, Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram) when publishing.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

TikTok and Instagram

TikTok data is handled under the TikTok Privacy Policy and Instagram data under the Instagram Privacy Policy. Access can be removed in each app's settings under connected or authorized apps.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while an account is connected and are deleted when the account is disconnected. Publishing records are kept for up to 12 months. To request deletion of any data, email us and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions: thegrailstreet@gmail.com.
