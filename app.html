The Grail Street

The Grail Street makes short videos about the stories and prices behind famous collectibles: trading cards, toys, sneakers, comics, coins and more. Find us as @thegrailstreet on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

The Grail Street Poster

The Grail Street Poster is our internal publishing tool. It lets our team schedule and publish The Grail Street's own videos to our own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts using each platform's official API. It is not offered to the public and is only used with accounts owned by The Grail Street.

How it works: a team member connects our account through the platform's official login, uploads a finished video with its caption and title, chooses a time, and the tool publishes it at that time. Video, caption, title and privacy settings are always chosen by our team before publishing.
